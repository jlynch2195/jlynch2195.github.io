Mentorship & Outreach

I am passionate about enabling students to pursue independent research projects outside of the classroom, where their scientific interests can really thrive. That is how I got my start as a scientist!

Mentorship

Below are projects by some amazing students whom I mentored in preparation for high school science fair competitions, including the Junior Science and Humanities Symposium (JSHS), the Central Western Oregon Science Expo (CWOSE), the Northwest Science Expo (NWSE), and the Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair (ISEF):

  • Alexis C., "Predicting Onset of Delayed Radio Emission in TDEs", in prep for 2027 CWOSE
  • Zoe K., "Examining Properties of Galaxies Producing Radio-Emitting TDEs", poster presentation at 2025 CWOSE. Awarded 3rd place, ISEF invitation, and $36,000 scholarship to the University of Oregon
  • Ella H., "The Typicality of the Sun Compared to Other Stars", poster presentation at 2025 NWSE. Awarded 2nd place, ISEF invitation, and $2,000 scholarship to Oregon State Univeristy
  • A.J., "Programmable Drones for Rapid Location of Avalanche Survivors", oral presentation at 2022 JSHS
  • Sophie C., "Discovering Optical Counterparts to X-ray Sources in Omega Centauri", oral presentation at 2021 JSHS

I have also have the privilege to mentor several UO undergraduate students on various radio astronomy projects:

  • Brody S., "From Visibilities to Flux: An Automatic Self-Calibration Pipeline", Summer 2026. Funded by Oregon Pathways to Industry Research Careers (OPIRC) Fellowship
  • Grace N., "Correlations Between Multiwavelength Properties of TDEs", Summer 2026. Funded by Vice President for Research and Innovation (VPRI) Fellowship
  • Cat A., "Improved Spectral Modeling for Radio TDES", Summer 2025. Funded by Mathematics and Science Teachers Engaging in Research to Inspire Teaching (MASTERIt) Scholarship

Outreach

I served as the Mentorship Coordinator for UO's Physicists for the Advancement of Gender Equity in Science (PAGES) from 2023-2025. In this role, I helped pair undergraduate physics majors with ~20 graduate student mentors based on shared identities and experiences, with the goal of increasing support for underrepresented groups in STEM.
